# Hotel Itoya: how to edit your website

Everything below is done in your **Studio** (the editing page for your website). Change something, press **Publish**, and the website, the chatbot, the booking form and the booking emails all update together, usually within a minute.

Nothing you change here can break the website. If a box is left empty, or something is entered that does not look right (a price of "5", a broken link), the website quietly keeps its previous value for that one box.

---

## Site Settings (the first item in the left menu)

One page with the facts that appear all over the website. Change one and it changes everywhere it is shown:

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Phone numbers** | Footer, Contact, Location, the chatbot's "call us" buttons, the privacy page, booking emails, receipts. The first number is shown first. Up to 5. |
| **WhatsApp number** | Every "WhatsApp us" button. Country code first, numbers only (example: `254714302777`). |
| **Email, street, town** | Shown in the footer, Contact, Location and booking emails. |
| **Times and hours** | Reservations team hours, front desk hours, check-in and check-out times, breakfast / lunch / dinner hours. The chatbot answers questions about these using what you type here. |
| **Social media** | The Facebook and Instagram buttons in the footer. |
| **Online booking** | The on/off switch described below. |
| **Website look** | Whether the Christmas theme appears (it starts and ends by itself). |

### Pausing online booking
Full house? Renovation? Switch **Accept online bookings** to **off** and publish. Straight away, guests who press "Book" see your message instead of the form (with buttons to call or WhatsApp you), and the booking system refuses new bookings. Switch it back **on** and publish to reopen. Existing bookings are not affected.

## Rooms

There is one entry for each of the four bookable rooms (Standard, Deluxe, Super Deluxe, Executive). Open one to change:

### Rates: two groups, three meal plans

Each room has its rates in two boxes of the form:

| Group | Currency | Plans |
|---|---|---|
| **Rates for Kenyan residents** | Kenya Shillings (KES) | Bed & Breakfast, Half Board, Full Board |
| **Rates for international visitors** | US Dollars (USD) | Bed & Breakfast, Half Board, Full Board |

- A rate is the price **per night for the room**. It is what guests are shown **and charged**, and it updates the room cards, the room popup, the booking form, the chatbot and the booking emails together.
- **Leave a rate box blank** to keep the rate the website already has. Every room already has its Bed & Breakfast, Half Board and Full Board rates, except **Super Deluxe**, which so far has only Bed & Breakfast for Kenyan residents and no dollar rates. Fill in its other boxes when you are ready and the extra plans appear on the booking form.
- **Enter 0** to stop offering that plan.
- The booking form only shows the plans that have a rate. A guest picks *Kenyan resident* or *International visitor*, then a meal plan, and sees the total in the right currency. A room with no dollar rates at all (Super Deluxe, until you add them) shows "Please enquire" to international visitors.
- **International visitors reserve online and pay at the hotel.** They are never sent an M-Pesa request. The booking, the confirmation email and your admin list all show the dollar amount, the meal plan and "International".
- The hotel confirms a guest's residency at check-in with their ID or passport. International visitors upload their passport photo page (a second page, such as the visa, is optional).

### The rest of the room

- **Maximum guests**: the booking form will not accept more.
- **Available for online booking**: switch **off** when that room type is full or out of service. It then shows as *Unavailable* and cannot be booked online. Switch on again to reopen it.
- **Price note**, the room's **name**, **description** and **photos**. Leave description and photos empty to keep the ones already on the website.

If a guest is in the middle of booking when you change a rate, they are not charged the old price by surprise: they are told the rate has been updated, shown the new total, and asked to confirm.

You cannot create a fifth room type or a second entry for the same room. The Studio will tell you if you try.

## Everything else you can edit

| Menu item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Gallery Images** | Photos in the gallery. They appear first, ahead of the built-in ones. |
| **Dining Menu** | Dishes and their prices. The menu on the website shows exactly what you add here. |
| **Special Events** | Events and offers shown in the Events section. |
| **Testimonials** | Guest reviews. The section stays hidden until you add at least one. |

---

## Not editable here (ask your developer)

- The longer descriptive paragraphs on the page (About, Conference, Reception, Parking and so on).
- The short description Google shows in search results, and the pre-written price range inside it.
- The standalone "page not found" screen.
- Opening the Studio for the first time, publishing a new version of the Studio itself, and where booking alerts are delivered.
